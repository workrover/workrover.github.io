The idea

Better environments. More useful feedback.

Train cowork agents by improving the environments they learn from.

Abstract

Scaling interactive environments has become increasingly important for training long-horizon agents through reinforcement learning. These environments often require further testing and refinement after initial generation, and correcting defects in a task may also require updating its work context and verifier. However, existing construction pipelines offer limited support for such iterative refinement while keeping these components aligned.

We introduce WorkRover, a framework for training long-horizon cowork agents through recursive environment evolution. Harness-driven synthesis jointly builds and validates semantically aligned work contexts, executable tasks, and evidence-grounded verifiers. Factorial Compatibility Replay (FCR) evaluates combinations of component revisions and selects the smallest validated repair among tested candidates. Verifier-to-Turn Credit Assignment (VTCA) augments trajectory-level GRPO advantages with deterministic workspace-progress signals and assigns a bounded share of positive credit to prerequisite turns.

We post-train Qwen3.5-35B-A3B to obtain WorkRover-35B-A3B, improving over its backbone by 26.27 percentage points on JobBench, 29.32 points on OfficeQA Pro, and 413.25 Elo points on GDPval v2.

FCR · Environment evolution

Repair the components together

A task failure can reveal a mismatch across its files, instructions, execution harness, or verifier. FCR freezes repair proposals, replays their combinations with dependency-induced updates, and checks the resulting environments.

VTCA · Turn-level learning

Give credit to checked progress

Frozen deterministic checks compare workspace states before and after each turn. Positive progress stays primarily with the turn that produced it, with a bounded share assigned to earlier turns whose exact tool outputs or artifact versions were used. Negative increments stay local. These signals shape GRPO advantages without intermediate judge calls.
